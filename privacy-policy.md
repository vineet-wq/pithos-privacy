# Privacy Policy — Pithos

**Last updated:** 3 October 2026

## Summary

Pithos does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on
external servers. Everything the app does happens on your device.

## What the app accesses

To find duplicate files and identify unused storage, the app reads:

- **Photos, videos and audio** on your device's shared storage
- **Documents and other files** in shared storage
- **File names, sizes and modification dates**
- **Storage usage figures**, including per-app usage if you grant usage
  access

The app reads this information only while a scan is running or when you
ask it a question. It is used to show you results and is not sent
anywhere.

## What the app stores

The following is stored on your device only, in the app's private
storage, and is removed when you uninstall the app:

- **Conversation history** from the assistant
- **Image fingerprints** used to detect similar photos
- **Scan results** — counts and sizes, not file contents
- **Your settings**

## Network use

Pithos connects to the internet for one purpose: downloading language
model files from Hugging Face when you choose to install a model. No
other network requests are made.

The assistant runs entirely on your device. Anything you type into it is
processed locally and is never transmitted.

## Permissions and why they are needed

**All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)**
Duplicate detection requires reading and comparing files across your
device's storage. Scoped storage access does not permit this, because
the app must be able to read files it did not create in order to compare
them.

**Photos, video and audio access**
Needed to list and compare media files.

**Usage access (optional)**
If granted, lets the app show how much storage other apps are using. The
app works without this; storage figures are simply less complete.

**Notifications**
Used to tell you when a background scan has found files worth removing.

**Internet**
Used only to download model files.

## Deleting files

When you choose to delete files, they are permanently removed. The app
does not move them to a recycle bin and cannot recover them. Files
belonging to other apps are protected and cannot be selected.

## Children

Pithos is not directed at children under 13.
